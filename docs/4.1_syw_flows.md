## Sports You Watch Specific Flows

`LucraUiProvider.LucraFlow.MatchupDetails`
Launches the matchup details experience for any Lucra matchup id (sports or games). For SYW, pass the sports contest id you wish to surface so users can review lines, participants, and history.

`LucraUiProvider.LucraFlow.CreateSportsMatchup`
Starts the Sports contest creation flow. Users can configure spreads, totals, or head-to-head matchups based on the sports catalog enabled for your tenant.
