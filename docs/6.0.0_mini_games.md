# Mini Games
Here you'll find the relevant LucraFlows, LucraEvents and related headless functions to integrate Mini Games into your app.

Mini Games are webview-hosted casual games supplied by Lucra (e.g. `runaway-web`, `hoops-web`) that the SDK can launch in practice, head-to-head, free-for-all, or tournament mode. The host app either embeds the SDK-provided WebView via `LucraFlow.MiniGame` or owns rendering and uses `LucraClient.startMiniGame(...)` to obtain the iframe URL.

Mini Games must be enabled for your tenant. Please contact your Lucra representative to enable this for your account.

## Reward methods

Minigame matchup rewards are claimed and viewed through the same `claimReward` / `markRewardViewed` headless methods used for tournament rewards. See [Headless Functionality](1.2.9_headless_interactions.md#rewards-claim--view-experimental).
